Our Story

Empowering Businesses
To Trade Globally

Founded in 2019 by Mr. Aniket Mishra, Mani Exim Solutions was born out of a vision to simplify the complexities of international trade for Indian businesses. Based in Kalyan, Thane, Maharashtra, we have grown into a trusted name in the import-export ecosystem.

Our team of seasoned professionals brings deep expertise in trade regulations, DGFT compliance, customs procedures, and freight logistics. We provide tailored solutions that help businesses confidently enter and expand in global markets.
